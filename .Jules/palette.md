## 2024-05-18 - Notebook Plot Accessibility & Clarity
**Learning:** Model accuracy plots in Jupyter Notebooks benefit significantly from distinguishable line styles, markers ('o' and 'x'), titles, labelled axes, gridlines, and clear legends to ensure accessibility for colorblind users and clarity in visual presentation.
**Action:** Always format matplotlib training/validation accuracy plots with standard title 'Model Accuracy', 'Epoch' on X-axis, 'Accuracy' on Y-axis, distinct markers/line-styles, and explicit legend placement.

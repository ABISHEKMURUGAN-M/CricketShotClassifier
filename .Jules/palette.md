## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Model Training Plots
**Learning:** Model training plots in notebooks often rely solely on color to distinguish between training and validation metrics, which is an accessibility barrier for colorblind users.
**Action:** Always use distinguishable markers (e.g., 'o' vs 'x') and line-styles (e.g., '-' vs '--') in addition to color when plotting multiple series in notebook visualizations to ensure they are accessible.

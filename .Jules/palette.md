## 2026-07-08 - [Accessible Data Visualizations in Notebooks]
**Learning:** Using distinguishable markers (e.g., 'o' and 'x') and line-styles (e.g., '-' and '--') in notebook plots is critical for colorblind accessibility, as color alone may be insufficient to distinguish training from validation curves.
**Action:** Always include markers, legends, and clear labels in model training plots.

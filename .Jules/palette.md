## 2025-05-14 - Distinguishable Markers and Line Styles in Notebook Plots
**Learning:** Using different markers (e.g., 'o', 'x') and line styles (e.g., '-', '--') in data plots is crucial for accessibility, allowing colorblind users to distinguish between data series even when colors appear similar.
**Action:** Always include markers and varying line styles in plots with multiple series to ensure colorblind accessibility.

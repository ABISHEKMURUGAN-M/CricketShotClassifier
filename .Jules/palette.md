## 2025-03-24 - Colorblind Accessible Plots in Notebooks
**Learning:** Using distinguishable markers and line-styles (e.g., 'o-' for training and 'x--' for validation) in notebook plots ensures accessibility for colorblind users who might otherwise struggle to differentiate series by color alone.
**Action:** Always use unique markers and line styles in addition to color when plotting multiple data series in Jupyter Notebooks.

## 2026-03-23 - Accessible Matplotlib Plots in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Default Matplotlib plots relying solely on line color differentiation are inaccessible to colorblind users and lack necessary visual context without explicit titles, axis labels, and legend formatting.
**Action:** Always include explicit titles, labeled axes, and distinguishable markers with varying line styles (e.g., solid with circles for training vs. dashed with x-markers for validation) in notebook model evaluation plots.

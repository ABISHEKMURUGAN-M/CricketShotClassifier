## 2025-08-08 - Accessible Plots and Transparent Model Outputs in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Colorblind accessibility in data visualizations (like training history and histograms) requires distinguishable marker shapes, line styles, explicit titles, and labeled axes with units rather than relying on default color cues alone. Furthermore, deprecations in model output predictions (such as Keras's `predict_classes`) should be modernized and outputs should be decoded using inverse transforms into human-readable labels to maintain usability and clarity.
**Action:** When designing or refactoring charts and outputs in Jupyter Notebooks, always use distinctive markers ('o' vs. 'x') and line-styles ('-' vs. '--') for different data series, explicitly label all axes, and decode model outputs into intuitive, human-readable labels rather than raw indices.

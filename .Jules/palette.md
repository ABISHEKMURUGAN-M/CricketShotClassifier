## 2026-07-15 - [Distinguishable markers in notebook plots]
**Learning:** For colorblind accessibility in notebook plots, it's crucial to use distinguishable markers and line-styles in addition to color to differentiate data series.
**Action:** When creating or modifying plots in Jupyter Notebooks with multiple series, always use different markers (e.g., 'o', 'x') and line-styles (e.g., '-', '--') and provide a legend.

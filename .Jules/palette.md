# Palette's Journal

## 2024-05-20 - Accessible Matplotlib Plots in Notebooks
**Learning:** Default Matplotlib plots lack labels, titles, legends, and distinguishable markers/line-styles, making them inaccessible to visual and colorblind users.
**Action:** Always include human-readable axis labels, title, legend, and distinguishable markers ('o' with '-' vs 'x' with '--') in Jupyter Notebook plot outputs.

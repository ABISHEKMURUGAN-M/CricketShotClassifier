## 2025-05-18 - Accessible Line Plots in Data Science Notebooks
**Learning:** Standard Matplotlib line plots relying solely on default colors are inaccessible to colorblind users. Combining distinct markers ('o' vs 'x') and line styles ('-' vs '--') alongside explicit titles, axis labels, and legends significantly enhances plot readability and accessibility.
**Action:** Always include distinct markers, line styles, explicit axis labels, titles, and legends when updating or creating Matplotlib line charts in Jupyter Notebooks.

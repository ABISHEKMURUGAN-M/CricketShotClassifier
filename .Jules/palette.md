## 2024-07-28 - Colorblind Accessibility in Jupyter Notebook Plots
**Learning:** Standard line plots in Jupyter Notebooks that display multiple series (like training vs validation accuracy) are often completely indistinguishable for colorblind users when using default line styles and colors. Using highly distinguishable markers and line-styles (e.g., 'o' with solid lines for training and 'x' with dashed lines for validation) ensures data remains accessible and clear. Proper labels, titles, and legends placed explicitly to avoid overlapping data points are also essential for visual clarity.
**Action:** Always add distinctive markers (like 'o' or 'x') and line styles (like solid vs dashed) when plotting multiple series in Jupyter Notebooks. Additionally, include descriptive labels, explicit titles, and properly positioned legends for optimal readability.

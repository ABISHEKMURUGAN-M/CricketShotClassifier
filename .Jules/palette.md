## 2024-05-16 - Colorblind Accessible Plots in Notebooks
**Learning:** Using distinguishable markers and line-styles in plots (like 'o' with '-' vs 'x' with '--') is critical for accessibility, allowing users with color vision deficiencies to differentiate between training and validation data.
**Action:** Always enhance default matplotlib plots in notebooks by adding legends, titles, and unique markers/line-styles.

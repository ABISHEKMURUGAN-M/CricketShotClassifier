## 2026-03-31 - Model Plot Accessibility in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Default Matplotlib plots in Jupyter Notebooks often lack distinguishable markers, line-styles, labels, and legends, making them difficult to read for colorblind users or when exported.
**Action:** Always include explicit titles, axis labels, distinguishable markers ('o' vs 'x'), line styles ('-' vs '--'), and legends in notebook plots.

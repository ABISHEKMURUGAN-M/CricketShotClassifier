## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Data Visualization in Notebooks
**Learning:** Using distinguishable markers and line styles in notebook plots (e.g., 'o-' for training and 'x--' for validation) ensures that information remains accessible to colorblind users who might not be able to differentiate based on color alone.
**Action:** Always use distinguishable markers, line-styles, and legends in data visualization plots within notebooks.

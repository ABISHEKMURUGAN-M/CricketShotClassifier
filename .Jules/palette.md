## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Data Science Notebooks
**Learning:** UX in technical notebooks is often overlooked. Providing human-readable mappings and descriptive, accessible visualizations (using markers and line styles, not just color) significantly improves the tool's utility for both developer and end-user.
**Action:** Always ensure model outputs are decoded into human-readable labels and plots include basic accessibility features like legends, titles, and distinguishable line patterns.

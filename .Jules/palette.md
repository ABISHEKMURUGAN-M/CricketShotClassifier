## 2025-05-18 - Accessible Line Plots in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Line plots with default matplotlib styles rely solely on color to distinguish data series (e.g. training vs validation accuracy), creating accessibility barriers for colorblind users and low-contrast environments.
**Action:** Always include distinguishable markers (e.g. 'o' vs 'x') and line styles (e.g. '-' vs '--') alongside explicit titles, axis labels, and legends.

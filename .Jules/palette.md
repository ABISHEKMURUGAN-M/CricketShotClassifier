## 2026-07-09 - Accessibility in Notebook Plots
**Learning:** Using distinguishable markers ('o', 'x') and line-styles ('-', '--') in addition to color ensures that plots are accessible to colorblind users. Adding titles, axis labels, and legends also significantly improves the intuitive understanding of model metrics.
**Action:** Always use markers and varied line-styles when plotting training/validation curves in notebooks. Ensure every plot has a title, axis labels, and a legend.

## 2024-09-29 - Plot Accessibility in Notebooks
**Learning:** Model accuracy plots in Jupyter notebooks require distinguishable line styles ('o-' vs 'x--'), titles, axis labels, and lower-right legends so users with visual impairments or colorblindness can easily distinguish training vs validation metrics.
**Action:** Always include explicit markers, line-styles, labels, titles, and appropriate legend placements in notebook visualizations.

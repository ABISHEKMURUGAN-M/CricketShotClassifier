## 2024-05-18 - Accessible Matplotlib Plots in Notebooks
**Learning:** Model accuracy plots need distinguishable markers ('o' vs 'x') and line styles ('-' vs '--') alongside clear legends and titles to be accessible for colorblind users and screen reader context.
**Action:** Use standard title 'Model Accuracy', Y-axis 'Accuracy', X-axis 'Epoch', legend at 'lower right', and distinct markers/line styles when plotting accuracy curves.

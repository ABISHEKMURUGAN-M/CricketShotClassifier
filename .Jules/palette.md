## 2024-07-30 - Accessible Visualizations in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Standard matplotlib and pandas histogram outputs lack the high-contrast elements, distinguishable markers/line styles, and descriptive metadata (explicit titles, units on axis labels) necessary for accessible and intuitive data science reporting. For example, using different markers and line styles (like 'o' with '-' and 'x' with '--') makes line charts accessible to colorblind users, while clear borders and descriptive titles make histograms more readable.
**Action:** When adding or updating visual plots in notebooks, always provide distinct markers/line-styles for multiple curves, include a descriptive title, specify clear units on both the X and Y axes, choose high-contrast and distinguishable fill/stroke colors, and disable cluttering gridlines when possible.

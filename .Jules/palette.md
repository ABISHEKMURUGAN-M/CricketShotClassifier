## 2026-11-09 - Accessible Line Plots in Jupyter Notebooks
**Learning:** Default line plots in Jupyter Notebooks that distinguish training vs. validation series purely by color are inaccessible to colorblind users and lack context without explicit titles, legends, and axis units.
**Action:** Always combine distinguishable marker shapes ('o' vs 'x') and line styles ('-' vs '--') alongside explicit titles, axis labels, and labeled legends positioned to avoid data overlap.

## 2025-08-03 - Jupyter Notebook Accessibility and Usability Standards
**Learning:** Visualizations in data science notebooks often lack title context, legends, proper colors, and axis units, which makes them inaccessible (especially for colorblind users). Standardizing training plots with distinguishable marker styles ('o' with '-' vs 'x' with '--') and legends, and adding explicit titles, colors, and labeled axes with units (e.g., 'Sequence Length (Frames)', 'Frequency (Count)') to histograms significantly improves data readability and accessibility.
**Action:** When working on notebooks, always ensure all plots have distinct styles, titles, legends, and clear labels with units on all axes.
